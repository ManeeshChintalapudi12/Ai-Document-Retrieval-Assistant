Enterprise Incident Response Procedure

Severity Levels

SEV-1:
Critical production outage affecting most customers.

SEV-2:
Major functionality unavailable with significant customer impact.

SEV-3:
Limited functionality affected with available workarounds.

SEV-4:
Low-impact issue with minimal customer impact.

SEV-1 Response

The on-call engineering team must acknowledge a SEV-1 incident within
15 minutes.

The incident commander coordinates engineering, infrastructure,
security, and business teams.

Communication

Status updates should be provided throughout major incidents.

Resolution

After service restoration, engineering teams must identify the root cause.

Post-Incident Review

SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents require a post-incident review documenting:

- Incident timeline
- Root cause
- Customer impact
- Resolution
- Corrective actions
- Prevention measures
