Enterprise Employee Handbook

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Employees must remain available during core business hours from 9:00 AM to
4:00 PM local time.

Paid Time Off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off annually.
Unused PTO may carry over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year.

Security Requirements

Employees must use company-managed devices when accessing internal systems.
Multi-factor authentication is required for all enterprise applications.

Password Policy

Passwords must contain at least 12 characters and should not be shared with
other employees. Passwords must be changed immediately if compromise is
suspected.

Equipment

The company provides approved laptops and necessary software for employees.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security immediately.
